AudioSyncMaster — visual redesign

A calm, guided single column. Every screen from the current app is here, rebuilt: nothing is removed, only re-ranked. The spine is a four-step flow — Select → Review → Analyse → Fix — and everything secondary (history, console, settings, updates) moves into overlays so the main surface stays quiet.

10

The system, in short

What actually changes, and the rules that keep it consistent as the app grows.

Principles
One accent, used once per screen.

Blue marks the single next action. Everything else is neutral, so the eye always lands in the right place.

Colour means status, never decoration.

Green/amber/red appear only on confidence, drift and failure — the three things that need judgement.

Numbers get the weight.

Delay is the largest text in a row. Filenames truncate; measurements never do.

Secondary tools live in overlays.

History, console and settings are summoned, not permanently docked, so the main column stays calm.

Every dead end explains itself.

Disabled buttons, failed rows and empty states say what to do next.

Tokens
radius — 6 / 9 / 13 / 18 px
space — 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 22 (4px base)
type — 11 · 12 · 12.5 · 13 · 14 · 22, system UI
